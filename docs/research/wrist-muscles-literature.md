# Wrist muscles (FCR, ECRL/ECRB): verified literature for motor-unit pool presets

Compiled 2026-11-05. Abstracts come from PubMed E-utilities (efetch, rettype=abstract). Full texts come from PMC HTML pages, from E-utilities PMC XML, or from an open-access PDF (Mizuno 1994, institutional repository copy, read from the page image).

**Read levels:** **A** = abstract only. **FT** = I read the full text (the relevant passage or table).

Rules I followed:
- Dispersion is marked SD or SE only where the source says which. Otherwise it says "± (unstated)".
- I computed no means, ranges or conversions. Where only individual values exist, I list them.
- **NO VERIFIED DATA** means I found no number in anything I read. The searches I ran are listed with each gap.

**Could not open** (publisher returned 403/HTML challenge, no open access per Unpaywall, or PMC holds only a scanned PDF that would not download):

In practice, a preset can be built with verified human values for these fields only:
- FCR type proportions

## Conflicts and caveats

1. **The 407.9 N / 589.8 N values are not "Holzbaur 2005".** Saul 2015 states that the peak forces "reflect measured muscle volumes (Holzbaur et al. 2007b) and isometric strength (Holzbaur et al. 2007a) from 5 adult males" and are "new parameters", fit with a specific tension of 50.8 N/cm². Corie & Charles cite refs [35–37] together. I did not read the original Holzbaur 2005 force table. Cite the model as Saul 2015 / Holzbaur 2007.
2. **Specific tension disagrees about threefold.** The model fit is 50.8 N/cm². Direct human gracilis gives 171 kPa (17.1 N/cm²). PCSA × specific tension will not reproduce 407.9 N unless the 50.8 value is used. The app offers both (`joint.peakForce`): Saul 2015 or × 17.1 / 50.8 (FCR ≈ 137 N, ECR ≈ 199 N).
2b. **Passive wrist stiffness disagrees with the passive moments.** Drake & Charles 2014 (via Davidson & Charles 2017, FT): 0.992 N·m/rad from small perturbations, i.e. ≈ 0.94 N·m at 54°. Delp 1996 (A): near zero over the central 150°. The app offers both (`joint.passive`).
3. **PCSA disagrees between sources:**
   - FCR: Liu (compartments 2.48 + 1.66, n = 10, age 39) vs Hua 1999 (1.90 whole, n = 6) vs a 104-yr cadaver (1.7)
   - ECRB: Liu (2.72 + 1.60) vs Abrams 2005 (3.3) vs 104-yr (1.5)
   - Liu's footnote says SD, but the figure uses SEM.
4. **ECR data pool ECRL and ECRB.** The Marseille group (Schmied, Aimonetti, Romaiguère) recorded from "extensor carpi radialis muscles" without separating heads in the parts I read. Romaiguère 1991 names both heads.
5. **ECR "force threshold" is recruitment threshold in N of wrist force, not MU twitch force.** Do not use it as twitch peak force.
6. **Aimonetti ISIs are imposed** (subjects held units firing at about 10 Hz). They are not natural firing ranges. The printed slow/fast pair ISIs (slow 48.69 ms vs fast 103.94 ms) look counter-intuitive and are quoted as printed.
7. **FCR fibre-type data come from small samples** with huge spread (ST 14.6–65.0%, n = 7). Mizuno's "FTb" is an ATPase type, which is not the same as MHC-IIX. Gantelius data are from children (7–13 yr) with an acutely immobilised fractured arm, and the pooled "flexor/extensor" means mix FCR with FCU and ECRB with ECU.
8. **Behrens twitch data are pooled wrist flexors** (FCR + other median-innervated muscles: PL, FDS). CT and HRT depend on joint angle (CT 63–68 ms, HRT 52–63 ms).
9. Animal data (cat FCR: Botterman, Gonyea) and other forearm muscles (EDC, FCU, EIP, APL/EPB, FDI) were found and **excluded** from the values above, except where a row is explicitly flagged.
10. **Abstract-only values** (A) were not checked against tables. These include Søgaard, Woods, Foley, Date, Humphries, Romaiguère 1991, Schmied 1994/1997, Calancie, Riek, Packman-Braun, Hartkopp, Holzbaur 2007a, Binder-Markey and Hosapatna.
11. Kuhn 2010 (PMID 20071267, A) is an FE/nerve model of forearm surface stimulation (electrode size, fat 0.25–2 cm, nerve depth 0.1–1.1 cm). It contains no muscle MU parameters.
